# CHAD subset

The clips in `videos/` and the frame labels in `labels/` are an unmodified subset
(56 clips from cameras 1 to 3) of the Charlotte Anomaly Dataset (CHAD), published by
TeCSAR-UNCC at https://github.com/TeCSAR-UNCC/CHAD under the Apache License 2.0.

If you use them, cite the CHAD paper: Danesh Pazho et al., "CHAD: Charlotte Anomaly
Dataset", SCIA 2023.

`gate*.json` are fitted by this project from those clips.
